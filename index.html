Privacy Policy — Knot Theory

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Knot Theory ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy completely. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Knot Theory does not ask for an account, a login, an email address, or any personal information. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

How the app works

Knot Theory runs entirely offline on your device. Your game progress — such as completed levels, stars, and your Daily Knot streak — is saved only in local storage on your own device. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. No data is shared with any third party because no data is collected.

Children's privacy

Knot Theory is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: admin@pharosventures.io.
